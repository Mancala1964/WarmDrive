WARM DRIVE - install on your Mac
================================

1. Open Terminal (press Cmd+Space, type "Terminal", press Return).
2. Type:  bash   followed by a space (don't press Return yet).
3. Drag "install.sh" from this folder into the Terminal window.
4. Press Return.
5. Quit GarageBand completely (Cmd+Q) and open it again.

In GarageBand:
- Select an audio track (or a software instrument track).
- Open Smart Controls (press B) > Plug-ins.
- Click an empty slot > Audio Units > Ortiz > Warm Drive.

If it doesn't show up:
- GarageBand > Settings > Audio/MIDI: make sure "Enable Audio Units" is ticked.
- Restart the Mac once (this forces a full Audio Unit re-scan).
